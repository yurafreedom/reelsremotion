The shortlist was limited to three existing project tracks: `Connection — Wayne Jones`, `Meridian — National Sweetheart`, and `No One Here Gets In Alive — National Sweetheart`. The third was rejected because the historical Reel 2 builder explicitly documents sung vocals; its HPF derivative was rejected for the same reason. `Connection` was selected because it is an established instrumental interview bed, has enough duration to run continuously, and its restrained electronic motion fits the reality/evidence theme without melodrama.

All five spoken clips use original 1.0x video and audio. Existing V3 subtitle and graphic windows were restored from their 1/1.5 timing scale to the original source clock; the real English question uses the approved contextual Russian translation split into four phrase-group windows, with derived visual reveal rather than fabricated Russian acoustic timestamps.
